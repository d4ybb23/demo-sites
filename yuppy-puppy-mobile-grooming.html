About Yuppy Puppy Mobile Grooming

With over 8 years of professional grooming experience, Yuppy Puppy Mobile Grooming brings expert care directly to your home. We understand that every dog is unique, and we treat each pup with the love, patience, and expertise they deserve.

Our mobile grooming service eliminates stress for anxious dogs while providing you with convenience. No more car trips or waiting rooms— just a professional groomer arriving with everything needed to make your dog look and feel their absolute best.

We specialize in working with dogs and cats of all ages and temperaments, using only premium, pet-safe grooming products and gentle handling techniques.
